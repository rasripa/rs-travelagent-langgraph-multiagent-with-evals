# Delay Repay Compensation

All compensation rules here are synthetic examples for demonstration purposes.

## What Delay Repay covers

Delay Repay compensates you when you arrive at your destination later than
scheduled, regardless of the cause of the delay. You do not need to show that
the operator was at fault.

Compensation is based on the **delay to your arrival** at your final
destination, not on the departure delay.

## Compensation bands

| Delay on arrival | Compensation | Calculated on |
|---|---|---|
| 15 to 29 minutes | **25%** | single fare paid |
| 30 to 59 minutes | **50%** | single fare paid |
| 60 to 119 minutes | **100%** | single fare paid |
| 120 minutes or more | **100%** | total fare paid, including the return leg |

Delays of **under 15 minutes do not qualify** for compensation.

## How the amount is calculated

Compensation is always calculated on the fare **you actually paid**, after any
railcard discount. It is not calculated on the undiscounted fare.

Worked example: a journey booked at an Off-Peak fare of 45.00 GBP with a
one-third railcard discount costs 29.97 GBP. If that journey arrives 47 minutes
late, it falls in the 30 to 59 minute band, so compensation is 50% of 29.97 GBP,
which is **14.99 GBP**.

## Claiming

- Claims must be submitted within **28 days** of the delayed journey.
- You need your booking reference and the date of travel.
- One claim per booking reference per journey. A booking already claimed against
  cannot be claimed again.
- Compensation is paid by the original payment method by default. Bank transfer
  and rail vouchers are also available.
- Claims are normally processed within 10 working days.

## Cancellations

If your booked service is **cancelled** and you choose not to travel, you are
entitled to a **full refund** rather than Delay Repay, and no administration fee
applies regardless of fare type, including Advance.

If your service is cancelled and you do travel on a later service, Delay Repay
applies in the normal way based on how late you arrive at your destination.

## Frequently asked

**Can I claim Delay Repay on an Advance ticket?**
Yes. Delay Repay applies to all fare types, including non-refundable Advance
fares.

**My train was cancelled and I travelled an hour later. What do I get?**
Compensation is based on your actual arrival delay. If you arrived 60 to 119
minutes late, that is 100% of the single fare paid.

**Does a missed connection count?**
Yes, provided the connection was part of the same booking and the delay to your
final arrival meets the 15-minute threshold.

**Can I claim twice for the same journey if both legs were late?**
No. Compensation is assessed once, on the delay to your final destination.
