# Railcards

All railcard details here are synthetic examples for demonstration purposes.

## Discount

All railcards listed below give **one third off** eligible fares, applied at the
time of booking. The discount is calculated on the adult single fare before any
booking fee.

## Available railcards

### Two Together Railcard

- **Discount:** one third off for both travellers.
- **Eligibility:** two named adults, both named on the card.
- **Key condition:** both named cardholders **must travel together** on every
  journey where the discount is used.
- **Time restriction:** applies to **Off-Peak and Anytime fares only**, which
  cannot be discounted for departures before 09:30 Monday to Friday.
  **Advance fares are unrestricted** and may be discounted on any service.
- Valid for one year from purchase.

### 16-25 Railcard

- **Discount:** one third off.
- **Eligibility:** aged 16 to 25, or a mature student in full-time education.
- **Minimum fare:** a minimum fare applies to **Anytime** tickets for journeys
  departing before 10:00 Monday to Friday. The minimum fare does not apply to
  Advance or Off-Peak fares, nor at weekends or on public holidays.
- Valid for one or three years.

### Senior Railcard

- **Discount:** one third off.
- **Eligibility:** aged 60 or over.
- **Time restriction:** no discount on Anytime fares before 09:30 Monday to
  Friday.

### Disabled Persons Railcard

- **Discount:** one third off for the cardholder and one adult companion.
- **Eligibility:** holders who meet the published disability criteria.
- **Time restriction:** none. The discount applies at all times, including the
  morning peak.

## Frequently asked

**Does my railcard discount apply to Advance fares?**
Yes. Advance fares can be discounted with any of the railcards above, and the
morning time restrictions do not apply to Advance fares.

**What happens if I travel without the railcard I booked with?**
The discounted ticket is not valid. You may be asked to pay the full undiscounted
fare for your journey.

**Can two railcards be combined on one booking?**
No. Only one railcard discount may be applied per ticket.

**Does the Two Together discount still apply if only one of us travels?**
No. Both named cardholders must be present. If only one travels, the discounted
ticket is not valid.

**Is the discount applied to the Delay Repay compensation I receive?**
Compensation is calculated on the fare **you actually paid**, which is the
discounted amount. See `delay-repay.md`.
