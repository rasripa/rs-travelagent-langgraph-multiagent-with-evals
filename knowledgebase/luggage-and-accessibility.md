# Luggage, Bicycles and Accessible Travel

All rules described here are synthetic examples for demonstration purposes.

## Luggage allowance

- **Three items per passenger**: two large items plus one small item of hand
  luggage. There is no weight charge.
- Maximum size for a large item is 30 x 70 x 90 cm.
- You must be able to carry and stow your own luggage. Staff are not able to
  lift luggage for you.
- Luggage must be stowed in the racks provided, in the vestibule racks, or
  above or below your seat. It must not block aisles, doorways or wheelchair
  spaces.
- Excess luggage may be refused if the service is busy.

### Items needing extra arrangements

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Skis, surfboards, golf clubs | Carried free if stowed safely, subject to space |
| Musical instruments | Large instruments may need a seat reservation purchased for them |
| Pushchairs and prams | Carried free; must be folded if the vestibule is congested |
| Mobility scooters | Permitted up to 120 x 70 cm and 300 kg including occupant |

## Bicycles

- **Folding bicycles** travel free on all services at any time, with no
  reservation, provided they are folded.
- **Non-folding bicycles** require a **free cycle reservation**, booked in
  advance. Spaces are limited, typically two to four per train.
- Non-folding bicycles are **not permitted on weekday peak departures**
  (06:30 to 09:29 and 16:00 to 18:59, Monday to Friday) on long-distance
  services.
- Electric bicycles are treated as non-folding bicycles. E-scooters are not
  carried.
- A cycle reservation is tied to a specific service. If your service is
  cancelled, the reservation moves with ticket acceptance but space on the
  replacement service is not guaranteed.

## Accessible travel

### Wheelchair spaces

- Every long-distance service in this demo has **one or two dedicated wheelchair
  spaces** in Standard class, each with an adjacent companion seat.
- Wheelchair spaces are reserved, not first-come. Book the space at the same
  time as the ticket.
- The reserved space appears on the booking as a seat of type
  `wheelchair space`, for example coach B, space WC1.
- Maximum wheelchair dimensions for the onboard space are 70 cm wide by 120 cm
  long.

### Assisted travel

- Assistance can be booked up to **2 hours before departure**. Booking further
  ahead is recommended so that staff are rostered at both ends of the journey.
- Assistance covers help getting to the platform, **ramp assistance for boarding
  and alighting**, help finding the reserved space or seat, and help at
  interchange stations during a connection.
- Assistance is recorded against the booking. When a booking carries assistance,
  any change or rebooking must re-confirm it on the new service, because
  assistance follows the service and not the ticket.
- Turn up and go assistance is available without booking at staffed stations,
  but cannot be guaranteed.

### Disruption and assistance

- A traveller with booked assistance whose service is cancelled is rebooked onto
  the next service **with a confirmed wheelchair space**, not merely the next
  service. A service showing zero wheelchair spaces available is not a valid
  alternative even if ticket acceptance is in place.
- Assistance must be re-confirmed on the replacement service, including at any
  new interchange station.
- Where no accessible alternative is available on the day, accessible onward
  transport is arranged at no cost to the traveller.

### Other provisions

- **Assistance dogs** travel free, with no reservation and no muzzle
  requirement. Up to two per passenger.
- **Other dogs and small pets** travel free, up to two per passenger. Dogs must
  be on a lead or in a carrier, and must not occupy a seat.
- Accessible toilets are provided on all long-distance services in this demo.
- Priority seating near doorways is available on request to passengers who need
  it, regardless of reservation.

## Frequently asked

**Can I take a non-folding bike on the 07:15 from Euston?**
No. 07:15 is inside the weekday morning peak window, so non-folding bicycles are
not carried on that departure. A folding bicycle is fine.

**My service was cancelled and I use a wheelchair. Am I just put on the next
train?**
No. You are rebooked onto the next service that has a confirmed wheelchair
space, and your ramp assistance is re-confirmed for that service at both ends.

**Do I pay for a wheelchair space?**
No. The space itself carries no charge. You pay the normal fare for the
journey.

**How late can I book assistance?**
Up to 2 hours before departure, though earlier is better so staff can be
rostered at every station you pass through.

**Is there a charge for extra luggage?**
No, there is no weight or excess charge, but carriage is subject to space and
you must be able to handle your own bags.
