# Fare Types and Rules

All fares described here are synthetic examples for demonstration purposes.

## Advance fares

Advance fares are the cheapest option and are released in limited quantities for
each individual service.

- **Valid only on the specific booked service.** If you miss your train, the
  ticket is not valid on a later one.
- **No changes permitted.** The booked service cannot be amended.
- **No refunds permitted.** The fare is non-refundable once purchased.
- Must be booked before the day of travel in most cases.
- Railcard discounts apply and are not time-restricted on Advance fares.

If your booked service is **cancelled or significantly delayed by the
operator**, the normal Advance restrictions are suspended. You may travel on an
alternative service at no extra cost, or claim a full refund with no
administration fee. This is an operator-caused exception and overrides the
no-change and no-refund rules above.

A delay counts as **significant** for this purpose at **60 minutes or more**. A
cancellation always counts, whatever the fare type. Shorter delays do not
suspend the fare restrictions, but may still qualify for Delay Repay once you
have travelled -- see `delay-repay.md`, where the threshold is 15 minutes.

## Off-Peak fares

Off-Peak fares offer moderate flexibility at a mid-range price.

- Valid on off-peak services only. See `ticket-validity.md` for peak definitions.
- **Changes are permitted**, subject to a **10 GBP administration fee** plus any
  difference in fare.
- **Refunds are permitted**, subject to the same **10 GBP administration fee**.
- Not valid on services departing within the morning or evening peak windows.

## Anytime fares

Anytime fares are the most expensive and the most flexible.

- Valid on **any** service on the date of travel.
- **Fully changeable with no fee.** Only a fare difference, if any, applies.
- **Fully refundable with no fee.**
- No time restrictions.

## Summary table

| Fare type | Refundable | Changeable | Admin fee | Tied to one service |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Advance | No | No | n/a | Yes |
| Off-Peak | Yes | Yes | 10 GBP | No |
| Anytime | Yes | Yes | None | No |

## Frequently asked

**Can I change an Advance ticket?**
No. Advance tickets are tied to a single booked service and cannot be changed or
refunded. The only exception is when the operator cancels or significantly
delays your booked service, in which case you may travel on an alternative
service or claim a full refund.

**What happens if I miss my Advance train?**
The ticket is no longer valid and a new ticket must be purchased. If the reason
you missed it was a delay on a connecting service covered by the same booking,
you are protected and should speak to staff.

**Is the administration fee charged per ticket or per booking?**
Per ticket.

**Can I upgrade from Standard to First after booking?**
On Off-Peak and Anytime fares, yes, subject to availability and the fare
difference. Advance fares cannot be upgraded; a new ticket must be purchased.
